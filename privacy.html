RanSan eStore uses the name, email, phone and optional billing information you provide to process your order, prepare invoices, provide product access and handle support.

Payments

Gateway payment information is processed by the selected payment provider. The store records order references, verification results and delivery status. Do not send payment credentials through support forms.

Optional updates and analytics

Choose email and WhatsApp product updates separately. You can change those choices through My purchases & preferences after email verification. Transactional purchase messages are separate from promotional updates.

Optional usage analytics records browser-session activity such as product views, cart additions and checkout starts when you opt in. The tracking events do not contain your name, email or phone.

Browser storage and access

The store uses browser storage for your cart, display preferences and saved order references. Verified customer access expires automatically. Close or lock access when using a shared device.

Questions or requests

Contact support@ransangroups.com for questions about your information, access, correction or deletion requests. Some order and invoice information may need to be retained for business records and applicable obligations.
