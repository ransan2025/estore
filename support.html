Find your purchase

Open RanSan eStore and choose My purchases & preferences. Verify the email used at checkout to view your orders, setup links and invoice previews.

Payment and delivery

Razorpay and Cashfree orders require server-confirmed payment before access is released. Direct UPI transfers require administrator verification of the bank credit. Do not pay again while an earlier payment is being checked.

Setup access and invoice emails are submitted after verification. A submitted message is not confirmation that it reached an inbox. Check Spam or contact support with your order number and payment reference. Never send a password, OTP or card details to support.

Compatibility and licence questions

Check the product requirements and licence terms before purchase. If details are missing or unclear, contact support before ordering.

Partners and affiliate offers

External partner offers open the seller’s website. Their price, stock, terms and checkout apply. RanSan may receive an affiliate commission. Contact support for partnership enquiries.
