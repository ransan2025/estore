✉ Need a hand?support@ransangroups.com

If you experienced a duplicate charge, failed delivery, incorrect product or access problem, contact support@ransangroups.com with the order number, payment reference and a description of the issue. Do not include card details or OTPs.

How requests are reviewed

RanSan reviews the verified payment, stated product terms, delivery/access status and the issue you report before communicating the available resolution. The product-specific terms shown for your purchase and applicable consumer rights govern the resolution.

Pending payments

If your payment is pending or verification is incomplete, contact support before paying again. A payment failure screen alone does not prove that a bank debit was reversed.

External seller purchases

For a purchase completed on a partner website, request support or a refund from that seller using its published policy.
